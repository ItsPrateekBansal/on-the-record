MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT — Acme Packaging Co.

This Agreement is made on January 1, 2026 between Harbor Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and Acme Packaging Co., 88 Carton Ave, Stockton, CA 95203 ("Supplier").

1. Scope and Term
1.1 This Master Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") governs all purchases by Buyer from Supplier between January 1, 2026 and December 31, 2026.
1.2 Each purchase is made by a written purchase order ("PO") issued by Buyer. Supplier shall reference the PO number on every invoice.

2. Pricing
2.1 The unit price for SKU AC-1212 (corrugated shipping box 12x12x8) shall be $1.20 per unit.
2.2 The unit price for SKU AC-TAPE (packing tape roll) shall be $3.40 per roll.
2.3 The unit price for SKU AC-WRAP (stretch wrap roll) shall be $18.75 per roll.
2.4 All prices in this Section 2 are fixed from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026 and include delivery to Buyer's Oakland facility.

8. Invoicing and Payment
8.1 Payment terms are net thirty (30) days from receipt of a correct invoice.
8.2 Supplier shall issue one invoice per shipment and shall not invoice the same goods more than once. A re-issued invoice must clearly state that it replaces an earlier invoice.
8.3 Buyer may dispute any invoice amount within thirty (30) days of receipt by written notice to Supplier. Undisputed amounts shall be paid on time.

9. Confidentiality
9.1 The prices in this Agreement are confidential information of both parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party.
